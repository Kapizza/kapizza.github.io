About

I hold a PhD in Computational Condensed Matter Physics from Binghamton University (GPA 4.00), where I developed algorithms for predicting stable compounds and superconductors using machine learning and evolutionary optimization — screening millions of candidate structures.

I am now applying my computational and analytical background to quantitative finance — building options pricing libraries, portfolio construction tools, and financial models.
