About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computational Condensed Matter Physics at Binghamton University. My research involves developing algorithms for predicting stable compounds and superconductors using computational techniques, including machine learning. Previously, I worked as an analyst-developer at the Bank of Georgia, gaining hands-on experience in financial systems and developing banking products.

Proficient in Python, PL/SQL, Java, and C++, I am eager to transition into the financial services industry, where I can apply my strong analytical and computational skills. My ability to tackle complex challenges through quantitative methods positions me well for roles in financial engineering, quantitative development and research.
